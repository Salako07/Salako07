## Featured research

### [robust-ecg-ssl](https://github.com/Salako07/robust-ecg-ssl) (in progress)
Does self-supervised pretraining still improve 12-lead ECG classification under scarce labels and distribution shift, once supervised baselines get the same augmentations? Zero-adaptation transfer from PTB-XL to the SPH hospital dataset, a 2 × 2 design with pre-specified hypotheses, and a full grid of 4 arms × 6 label budgets × 5 seeds. Baseline reproduces published PTB-XL results (fold-10 macro-AUROC 0.9228 vs 0.9242). `PyTorch` `SimCLR` `PTB-XL` `SPH`

### [Agentic-ECG-Interpreter](https://github.com/Salako07/Agentic-ECG-Interpreter)
Interpretable 12-lead ECG classification (~110 physiological features, XGBoost, test-fold macro-AUROC 0.867) paired with a LangGraph agent that retrieves cardiology references and writes a cited report with an auditable decision trace. Research prototype, not for clinical use. `XGBoost` `NeuroKit2` `LangGraph` `ChromaDB`

### [Cancer-Tumor-Classifier](https://github.com/Salako07/Cancer-Tumor-Classifier)
DenseNet vs ResNet transfer learning for three-class tumor image classification on an imbalanced dataset. `TensorFlow` `Keras`
